01 / ABOUT

A journey shaped by curiosity and service.

Gimono Grace was born on 23 February in Busia, Uganda. She is passionate about learning, technology, education, leadership and community development. Her journey reflects a commitment to using knowledge and leadership to solve practical problems and support the growth of others.

She is pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering at Busitema University, having previously completed a Diploma in Computer Engineering. Alongside her studies, she works in education administration and has participated in student and community leadership.

Her work also includes Christian ministry and the development of Balanced Youth Mentorship, an initiative that promotes healthier relationships, responsible leadership, gender partnership and stronger communities.
